# Hi, I'm Kim 👋

I'm Head of UX at [Alegeus](https://www.alegeus.com/), where I lead an AI-native team designing B2B2C healthcare and fintech products used by millions. I build the systems that let people and AI build together.

I learned to build in my family's sewing shop, fixing machines with my grandfather and sewing with my grandmother. Since then: NASA Mars research, Sandia National Laboratories, nearly 13 years in UX leadership at Fidelity, and now experience systems for people and AI agents.

### 🛠️ What I'm building

AI is changing how products get made, but designers still need to think clearly, work within strong systems, and know why they're building what they're building. I build systems and tools at the intersection of DesignOps, AI-native product development, and design system engineering, thinking, so designers can spend less time fighting process and more time doing their best work.

**[My New Portfolio Site](REPO-LINK): built in public**
A site that's also a working example of how I build: Astro and Vercel, design tokens, accessibility checks on every pull request, and conventions written for AI agents (`AGENTS.md`, `CLAUDE.md`).
*Status: In progress*

**AI-Native System in a Box**
An open-source starter kit for teams who want to design and build with AI from day one: design tokens, components with human- and machine-readable specs, a voice and content layer, agent conventions, accessibility and quality checks, and process templates for finding the right problem before building. I'm also scoring how well AI agents follow it.
*Status: In progress*

**Ask Kim: Less Scroll, More Substance**
Instead of making you dig through case studies, you'll be able to just ask. The assistant answers only from my own work, cites its sources, and admits when it doesn't know. It will also allow you to request my resume, formatted how you want it... PDF, TXT, HTML with my style, HTML with a random style. Alongside it, I'm running live experiments on how people explore the site, and I'll publish what I learn, including what doesn't work.
*Status: Planned*
