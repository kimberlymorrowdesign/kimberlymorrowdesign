### 💡 Why AI-Native Is Great News for Design

AI has changed how products are designed and built, but it has made design more important, not less. The things great designers do, AI can't do. The things great designers would rather skip, AI does really well.

AI can produce decent-looking artifacts in seconds. But artifacts (style guides, HTML files, journey maps, user research reports) have never been where design's real value lies. Great design happens mostly behind the scenes: making sure something works, solves a real need, is easy to use, *and* looks great.

That takes designers who keep learning the new tools of their trade (they've always changed, and they always will), think critically (especially with AI), solve hard problems, and build and work within strong systems that scale impact. Designers who understand the why behind what they're building, and who have the grit, the heart, and maybe a touch of delusional optimism to keep trying even when others say it's impossible. They decide what's worth building, direct how it gets built, and define how we'll know it's been built right.

They're also the changemakers. They help Product break out of requirements and Engineering break out of code. They push others to question everything and buck the status quo, because that's the only way to find new ways to solve new hard problems.

So yes, I can make you a lovely style guide (mood boards first, if you like), the persona sheets of your dreams, and journey maps you'll want to frame and hang over your couch. But what I'm really here for, what I put my heart into, and what you want me caring about a little more than is healthy, is building the systems you may never see but will definitely feel: where things get better, easier, faster, and smoother.

**AI-Native System in a Box**

Everyone says to go AI-native... but what does that mean, how do you do it, and where do you start? Right here. An open-source starter kit for teams who want to design and build with AI from day one: design tokens, components with human- and machine-readable specs, a voice and content layer, agent conventions, accessibility and quality checks, process templates, and sage advice from the trenches. The specs are scored on how well AI agents follow them.

*Status: In progress*

**Ask Kim: Less Scroll, More Substance**

Instead of digging through case studies (unless that's your thing... no judging), you'll be able to ask me, or at least a linted approximation of me, my knowledge, my work, and my style. The assistant will answer only from my own work, writing, and teaching, cite its sources, and admit when it doesn't know. It will also build you my resume in whatever format you like: PDF, ASCII, or HTML.
